Ask us anything ↗
Who can work with Truck Ventures?

We welcome conversations with owner-operators, independent drivers, and fleet owners. Tell us about your equipment and operation so we can discuss the right dispatch support.

Can I choose my preferred routes?

Yes. Your preferred lanes, home-time needs, and equipment are part of our planning conversation. Available freight and load details are discussed with you before a booking is confirmed.

What do I need to get started?

Start with your contact information, equipment type, and preferred routes. We’ll discuss your operation and walk you through any carrier documents needed for onboarding.

How much does dispatching cost?

Reach out to discuss your equipment, fleet size, and service needs. We’ll review pricing and service terms with you before you decide to get started.
